# Editorial standards for the portfolio and Content Studio

## Primary writing standard
Use AP Style conventions for U.S. English wherever appropriate. Favor accurate attribution, short sentences, precise verbs, consistent capitalization, plain language and specific evidence. The AP Stylebook itself is proprietary; this document is an original house-style summary, not a reproduction or substitute for the current licensed guide.

## Portfolio copy
Keep first-person claims factual and attributable. Use sentence case for explanatory prose, except proper nouns, product names and intentional display headlines. Use “email,” “website” and “U.S.” in prose. Avoid unverified superlatives, exaggerated performance promises, generic AI jargon and inconsistent date formatting. Preserve the names and titles of published works as they originally appeared; do not silently retitle third-party publications to enforce house style.

## Editorial grading
Treat portfolio examples as editorial craft references, not reusable copy or evidence for new claims. Evaluate hook specificity, audience fit, argument progression, evidence discipline, format conventions, usefulness and originality. Metadata-only references cannot establish true similarity or grade against a complete work.

## General U.S. legal preflight
Identify claims requiring substantiation, endorsements and material connections requiring disclosure, copyright and trademark concerns, regulated-industry assertions, privacy implications and potentially misleading pricing or savings statements. Refer potentially material issues to qualified counsel. The demo is not legal advice or approval.

## SEO and discoverability
Match search intent; use a descriptive, accurate title and heading hierarchy; answer the reader’s question clearly; avoid keyword stuffing; add helpful internal links and accessible image descriptions where appropriate. Optimize titles, meta descriptions, canonical and indexability settings at publication. Video, social and ad assets need channel-specific metadata and landing-page optimization rather than a generic blog SEO score. No heuristic score guarantees rankings.
